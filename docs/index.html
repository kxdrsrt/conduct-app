Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Auto Mode

Intelligently detects which music app is currently playing and routes keys there automatically.

YouTube Browser Control

Controls YouTube playback in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera, and more. 12 browsers supported.

Universal Media Key Routing

Play/pause, next, previous. Always directed exactly where you want them.

Volume Key Hijacking

Optionally redirect hardware volume keys to control your music app's volume instead of system audio.

9 Supported Players

Apple Music, Spotify, Doppler, VOX, Swinsian, Cider, DaftCloud, YouTube, and Auto mode.

Invisible & Lightweight

Lives in your menu bar as a tiny baton icon. Near-zero CPU and memory usage.

Launch at Login

Set it once, forget forever. Starts automatically when you log in to your Mac.

Fully Open Source

MIT licensed. No telemetry, no ads, no tracking. Inspect every line of code yourself.

How it works

Three seconds to set up.

1

Download & open

Drag Conduct to your Applications folder. Launch it and it appears in your menu bar.

2

Grant permission

Allow Accessibility access so Conduct can intercept media key events at the system level.

3

Pick your player

Choose your music app from the menu, or let Auto mode figure it out for you.
